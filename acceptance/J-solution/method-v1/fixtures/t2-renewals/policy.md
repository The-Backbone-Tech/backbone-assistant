# Renewal discount policy (synthetic)

1. A renewal whose proposed discount is above 15% is an exception and needs VP Sales approval.
2. A discount of exactly 15% or less is within policy.
3. An exception whose renewal date is within 21 days of the review date is urgent.
4. A renewal already signed is out of scope, whatever its discount.
5. Report the ARR at risk: the sum of ARR of all exceptions, and of urgent exceptions separately.
6. Never approve, send or promise anything in the brief: it is for the VP to decide.
