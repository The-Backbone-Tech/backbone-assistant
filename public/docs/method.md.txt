# How Backbone makes a choice

**What this page owns:** the method behind every Backbone recommendation, whether the choice is a tool for a company, a route for one task or a piece of Backbone's own architecture. It is doctrine: five steps, how to weigh considerations, and one worked example. **What it does not own:** the choices made with it ([decisions and references](decisions-and-references.md)), its runtime behaviour ([solution selection](specs/solution-selection.md)), the outcome ([GOAL](../GOAL.md)) or the rules for working on this repository ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)).

Permission belongs to the actual person or company. Development follows [contribution guidance](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Start with the requested outcome and give the agent the context, tools, authority and working environment needed to deliver it. Understand what already works and compare ways to complete the whole job, including their ongoing burden. Keep enough reasoning for people and later agents to improve the approach.

The method applies to a software team, a business function or a company adopting AI. A small change can be enough to finish a substantial job. Choose its size from the outcome, required capability and cost of ownership. Keep the full requested result in view when choosing what to reuse, adapt or build.

For example, an engineering team that produces patches quickly but loses time in review should examine how reviewers find the tested revision and the reason for a decision. A support team that writes good replies but misses incoming requests should examine how work starts and who sees it in time. Both may benefit from AI. The useful intervention follows the bottleneck, and the proof follows the accepted result. Backbone applies this method in its own configuration, architecture and guidance. Its repository split, work planes and Arena are ways it implements parts of the method.

1. **Start with what should improve over successive cycles.** Understand what people are trying to achieve, where they lose time or judgment today, and what the AI could usefully do on its own. A first success matters. So does whether the next person can continue, adapt to changed facts and make a better decision.
2. **Separate what must hold from how we make it hold.** The company's sources and rules, the operator's ability to correct the work, current authority and a way to resolve consequential uncertainty define the room the AI can use. The model, interface, deployment, cadence and division of work can vary inside it. Apply constraints to the actions they govern so a missing permission for one operation does not stop independent useful work.
3. **Choose for this company at this moment.** Begin with what already works, including people's habits, the capabilities they pay for and their capacity to operate a change. Recommend a concrete next step. A bundled tool, a specialist service, a small adaptation or a new arrangement can each win. Compare the whole result and burden over time. Apply the same standard to Backbone's own pieces and retire them when a better arrangement makes them unnecessary. [Solution selection](specs/solution-selection.md)
4. **Seek evidence that could change the choice.** Ask what would reverse the recommendation before choosing the investigation. A primary source can settle what a provider offers. A lab can isolate why a mechanism helps. A simulation can expose a consequence across a whole scenario. Arena compares what those observations support, with their dates, limitations and shared lineage. Use the smallest investigation that can distinguish the alternatives and make a clear recommendation when the evidence permits.
5. **Give people and agents enough reasoning to disagree and adapt.** Keep the reason for the choice, the assumption on which it turns, the plausible alternative and the evidence that would change it. A correction can improve today's work immediately. Generalizing it requires a new case where the proposal could be wrong. The same discipline keeps the repository understandable and gives old mechanisms and guidance a way to be retired.

A choice can be flexible in the shared product and binding in one installation. Base's D542 (`c7140d185`) used the consequence of getting a choice wrong to decide where deterministic enforcement paid for itself. Its examples were crossing a tenant boundary, escaping authority, corrupting accepted records and leaking secrets. The same reasoning leaves room for different interfaces, vocabulary and working methods. A company's policy can narrow that room for a specific operation. For example, an approval screen is generally replaceable. If a company requires independent review for one class of action, every eligible screen or channel must preserve that review. A preference for a familiar screen can still break a tie among routes that meet the requirement. Keeping these levels separate lets a policy be firm in context without turning a local preference into a universal product rule.

## Reconsider an inherited mechanism before repairing it again

**Owner direction, 10 November 2026; consolidated 13 November.** The [owning direction](../GOAL.md) asks what arrangement best completes the actual work. Repeated friction, changed capabilities or material upkeep are reasons to recover a component's original purpose. Distinguish the required property from a configuration choice, implementation, workaround or hypothesis. Compare repair with removal, configuring the incumbent, adopting a maintained component or changing the composition. Past effort and test count do not establish that a component belongs; settled working parts need no uniformity rewrite.

Keep the accepted requirement and its owner explicit. If a test encodes a superseded mechanism, retain its failure and reason, state the property that remains and qualify the replacement with relevant positive and adverse cases. A changed acceptance requirement needs a versioned disposition at its owner. Making an old test pass alone cannot establish that the product solves the right problem.

Compare the whole operating arrangement: setup, context transfer, duplicated records, human attention, correction, maintenance, recovery and exit, alongside model quality and spend. Existing software, a bounded migration and a small bridge are eligible routes. Keeping source systems is a useful default where it reduces disruption; it is not a prohibition on a migration that demonstrably improves the whole result. Scope controls to the actual operation and authority, so a personal configuration does not inherit unrelated enterprise machinery.

A new abstraction or cryptographic mechanism needs a stated consequence, alternatives and discriminating evidence. Future adaptability means replacing a component at acceptable cost while preserving work and unresolved obligations. It does not require implementing every possible future environment. The [research dispositions](decisions-and-references.md#research-dispositions-reviewed-13-november-2026) show which recent proposals were implemented, rejected or remain unproved.

## Remove obstacles to completing the work

**Design principle, clarified 5 November 2026.** Backbone should help capable AI carry an assignment through to its intended result. Designing the environment includes making necessary sources and tools discoverable, providing usable feedback, and letting the agent inspect, experiment, repair and change its approach within the applicable authority. Assess a constraint by the concrete consequence it protects and the useful work it still allows. Apply this reasoning to [positioning, organisation, structure, technology, protocols and abstractions](shape.md#enable-capable-work-at-every-level), as well as to individual tool calls.

When progress stops, find the actual cause. Missing knowledge calls for inspection or a focused question. An unfamiliar tool calls for discovery and an example. A broken environment calls for diagnosis and an authorised repair. An unavailable route calls for a different eligible route or a concrete setup step. A missing permission calls for the precise decision that would enable the affected operation. Continue independent work and retain the full assignment while resolving that dependency.

Use the strongest suitable native capabilities and let the agent choose its working method. Temporary scripts, working data, tools, parallel work or a change of model can help when their contribution warrants the effort. Preserve those options through adapters and interfaces. A fixed sequence, a reduced tool surface or a requirement to use one model needs evidence that it helps the actual job. Revisit a workaround when the model, client or company environment changes.

Carry existing authority forward. Give the agent enough scope for ordinary preparation, exploration and repair so the person decides meaningful changes and commitments. A decision goes to the person only when its answer changes the result or permits an action they have not signed. Put checks at the affected source, shared record or external action, and keep ordinary work moving inside that boundary. When an action is held, show the route to resolution and its responsible owner.

Make completion concrete in the artifact: its audience, required contents, factual basis and the action its recipient can take. Inspect those contents even when a format checker passes. Judge the arrangement by completed work and the effort left to people. Observe whether the agent finds an available capability, repairs a recoverable failure, continues after a tool changes, and reaches the requested result. Count unnecessary permission requests, manual rescue, repeated setup and abandoned work alongside errors and denied actions. A control earns its place when it protects the relevant consequence while allowing the intended work to finish. ## Applying the method while completing Backbone

The platform scope clarified on 5 November distinguishes the complete product from one company's adoption. The method chooses how to build, integrate and explain the full platform; it does not reduce development to a few novel capabilities or to what the first company enables. Replicating a reference and practising its mechanisms are valid development and learning work. Attribute sources, preserve licence obligations, distinguish experiments from qualified components and test any claimed improvement. [GOAL](../GOAL.md) owns completion and [Shape](shape.md#complete-platform-and-proportionate-configurations) owns platform requirements, configuration requirements, optional enhancements and alternatives.

## Which considerations carry the most weight

- **The outcome and the company set the question.** A new team may need a complete working setup. An established function may need only a reliable handoff or a supported connection. The same capability map describes both. Existing tools and licences matter where they supply the specific operation, account and rights the job needs.
- **Authority and the integrity of the work determine eligibility.** Keep working where permission and evidence suffice. Where a necessary source, action or recovery route is unavailable, report the actual limit and choose what can still be done. A better model or lower price does not repair a missing permission, an inaccessible edit or an unknown external result.
- **Results and operating burden distinguish eligible routes.** Compare task quality, useful autonomy, human judgment, continuity and effort in proportion to what matters on this job. A recurring review can justify a different arrangement from a one-off analysis. Use real deadlines and the cost of a missed action to decide how much control and interruption the work warrants.
- **Defaults and experiments move on different evidence.** A current default should be explainable and replaceable. New techniques, Arena challengers and simulation findings can justify a reversible trial. Changing the default for a supported environment calls for evidence that the complete result or its upkeep improves there. Preserve the reason for keeping or retiring the previous choice.

Apply the same weighting to research. Start with capabilities already solved by maintained products. Inspect how and why they work where it changes the choice. Preserve prior attempts as evidence of limits and missed assumptions. Treat the team’s development environment, specifications, work planes and tools for inspecting shipped software as possible contributions to a concrete outcome. Their weight depends on what the team needs and can sustainably operate. This supports comparing and composing before extending a prototype. Replicating a reference is one eligible route beside adopting, integrating, adapting and building, and where a reference is ahead it is taken without waiting for a comparison ([GOAL](../GOAL.md#ambitious-outcome)); the comparison decides what may be claimed afterwards.

Give evidence weight against a particular claim. A rising project can merit a look before it merits a trial. A strong benchmark can justify trying an executor without deciding what the company should change. A single reproducible counterexample can disqualify a route from a required property, even when it tells us little about the failure rate. Conversely, a clean small trial can justify the next reversible step while leaving general reliability open. Where the choice is close, spend effort on the uncertainty that would alter it. Where every feasible alternative has the same unresolved dependency, name that dependency and proceed with the useful work that is available. Evidence cannot be collapsed into one confidence score.

Best in class needs a stated unit of comparison. The strongest classifier, search tool and coordinator in isolation can form a weaker operating arrangement if the joins lose context or leave people coordinating the coordinators. Begin with the best feasible complete route. Substitute a specialist where its gain survives the handoff, and compare the changed arrangement through the recipient's accepted result. Try the most informative substitution first. If it only wins on the intermediate metric, keep the simpler route for this job. If it wins in a hard class of cases but adds burden in routine work, consider using it for that class and verify the routing decision. This extends the October OSS composed-work acceptance, which assesses source correctness, useful completion, effort and cost together while preserving native operation without the optional assistant. See `backbone-oss:4b8a8fce:docs/capability-parity.md`, “Easy integration and composed-work acceptance.”

Use the same reasoning when building Backbone, doing work through it and explaining a choice to another team. Public guidance, executed work and an optional service each earn their own evidence. Keep the rationale useful even when the receiving team adopts no Backbone software.

Commercial incentives must not bias design or recommendations. Commercial use, hosting and services are allowed, as clarified on 5 November. Backbone can lose a particular adoption comparison while development of the complete platform continues. Independence also needs to show in the method. Give incumbents, paid services and our own components the same job, eligibility constraints and standard of evidence. Account for switching in both directions. For an adversarial read, remove the supplier names and ask whether the reasons still support the same choice, then restore the names and check the obligations they actually carry, including support and exit. If the incumbent already meets the need, retaining it is a substantive recommendation. Say why it wins and which later change would make a specialist or a Backbone component worth adding. The team can reuse that judgment even when it adopts no software. See the [product goal](../GOAL.md#what-the-design-must-optimise) and `backbone-arena:docs/`, owner clarification of 4 November.

Consider the strongest case for using something else. A company may have an external arrangement that already delivers the whole job, with acceptable oversight, continuity and cost. Recommend it on that evidence. The company can still use Backbone's reasoning to reassess the choice later, and can reuse the public comparison without connecting its systems. If it needs help only with evaluation or a missing capability, compare that contribution on its own. If it needs a complete operating layer, compare the complete Backbone experience and the external arrangement on the same work. These are different claims of value. A useful recommendation is evidence that the judgment helped; it cannot establish that the product ran the job. Equally, an installed feature cannot establish that a company needed it. **Worked example (a desk check, not a measurement).** A maintenance team asks for AI to reduce alert interruptions. Imagine that the best available assistant can classify and summarize every incoming alert. The useful next step still depends on where the work breaks down.

| What the team observes | What to do next | What would change the choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The existing ticket rule reliably reaches the right responder. Repeated low-priority alerts consume attention. | Keep urgent routing and compare a sourced shift brief with the existing alert feed. Measure repeated interruptions and whether people can still spot an urgent event. | A deadline is missed, or preparing and checking the brief takes as much effort as reading the feed. |
| Urgent alerts are missed because different teams use different definitions and no one settles conflicts. | Agree on who owns severity and compare examples on which the rules disagree. Use the assistant to surface the disagreement and preserve the reasons for the decision. | A rule with an accountable owner covers the disputed cases reliably. Then automate its eligible routine application and reserve ambiguous cases for review. |
| The right person sees the alerts, but repairs stall at shift change because ownership and the last safe action are unclear. | Improve the handoff in the existing ticket record. Verify that the next shift can act from the latest confirmed state. | A later shift still loses the thread despite a complete handoff. Compare the best available coordination option on the actual cause of that loss. |

A missing reading and an instruction to act on equipment are separate cases. The first changes what the team knows. The second changes what it is authorizing. A simulation can exercise both during an alert burst without touching equipment. This desk check keeps model capability constant and varies the business cause. It yields three recommendations with different evidence and owners. An independent reader or agent should be able to reach a defensible choice on a new case and explain what would reverse it.
